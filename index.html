Help with Spot On

Spot On is a run of quick estimation and timing challenges: count the dots, mark the fraction, keep the beat. One miss ends the run.

Contact

Email constable-co.support@proton.me. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you have, and what you saw. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

Tempo says I'm early or late, but I'm on the beat.
Recalibrate in Settings → Recalibrate tap timing, using the same speaker or headphones you play with. Bluetooth headphones add delay, and calibration removes it. Sound on and Sound off keep separate calibrations.
Can I play without sound?
Yes. With Sound off, Tempo's beats are felt as light taps (Settings → Beat haptics, which needs Haptics on). With both off, the ring pulses on each beat.
I'm left-handed.
Settings → Left-handed moves the side drag handles to the left.
The animations bother me.
Spot On follows the iPhone's Reduce Motion setting (Settings → Accessibility → Motion): results appear with a short fade instead of moving.
How do I see the tutorials again?
Settings → Replay tutorials. Each challenge shows its short demo again the next time it comes up.
A challenge code doesn't work.
Codes only work between copies of the same version of Spot On. If a friend's code is refused, one of you may need to update the app.
How do I reset my best score and stats?
Everything is stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes it all.

Privacy

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your scores stay on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
