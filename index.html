Help with Spot On

Spot On is a run of quick estimation and timing challenges: count the dots, mark the fraction, keep the beat. One miss ends the run.

Contact

Email constable-co.support@proton.me. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you have, and what you saw. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

Tempo says I'm early or late, but I'm on the beat.
Recalibrate in Settings → Recalibrate tap timing, using the same speaker or headphones you play with. Bluetooth headphones add delay, and calibration removes it. Sound on and Sound off keep separate calibrations.
Can I play without sound?
Yes. With Sound off, Tempo's beats are felt as light taps (Settings → Beat haptics, which needs Haptics on). With both off, the ring pulses on each beat.
I'm left-handed.
Settings → Left-handed moves the side drag handles to the left.
The animations bother me.
Spot On follows the iPhone's Reduce Motion setting (Settings → Accessibility → Motion): results appear with a short fade instead of moving.
How do I see the tutorials again?
Settings → Replay tutorials. Home then reads LEARN TO PLAY again and walks you through the first six challenges, and every other challenge shows its tutorial again the next time it comes up. Each tutorial has a SKIP button.
A challenge code doesn't work.
Codes only work between copies of the same version of Spot On. If a friend's code is refused, one of you may need to update the app.
How do I remove the ads?
Remove ads is a one-time purchase, in Settings or from the small Remove ads link after a run. It removes the ads between runs for good, and the extra life after a miss becomes free, with no ad to watch.
What is the extra life?
Once per run, after a miss, you can watch a short ad to carry on from the same level with a new round, keeping your score and your SPOT ON streak. It's offered in normal runs and in challenges, never in the daily. A run that used one can still be sent as a challenge: your friend gets an extra life too.
What does Practice mode do?
It's a one-time purchase that lets you practise a single challenge for as long as you like, at any difficulty you've reached. Start it from PRACTICE THIS EVENT after a miss, or by tapping a challenge on the Stats screen.
I bought something and it's gone (new phone, reinstall).
Settings → Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with. Purchases are never charged twice.
How do I change my ad privacy choices?
Tracking is under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Settings → Privacy choices in the game reopens the consent form.
How do I reset my best score and stats?
Everything is stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes it all.

Privacy

No accounts, and your scores stay on your iPhone. Spot On shows ads through Google AdMob, personalised only if you allow it, and you can remove them with a one-time purchase. Read the privacy policy.
