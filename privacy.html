Privacy policy

Effective 25 October 2026. This policy covers the Spot On app for iPhone.

The short version: Spot On doesn't collect any information about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. What the game remembers stays on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. Spot On doesn't ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it has no sign-in. The app doesn't send your data to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your iPhone

To work as a game, Spot On saves a few things in its own storage on your device:

  • your best score;
  • your stats for each challenge (rounds played, pass rate, recent accuracy, best streaks) and the highest level of each you've reached for practice;
  • your daily challenge results and your daily streak;
  • your settings (sound, haptics, beat haptics, left-handed, tap-timing calibration) and which tutorials and new challenges you've already seen.

This information never leaves your device through Spot On. It may be included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud or computer), which Apple's and your own settings control. Deleting the app deletes it.

Sharing

When you tap Share or Challenge, Spot On opens the standard iOS share sheet with a short text, such as your score, level and a challenge code. Nothing is sent unless you pick where it goes, and then it's handled by the app you chose (for example Messages), under that app's privacy terms. A challenge code holds only the run's random seed, the game version, your score and the level reached.

Opening a Spot On challenge link (spoton://) starts that run on your device; the link isn't sent anywhere.

Advertising and tracking

Spot On shows no ads, contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, and doesn't track you across apps or websites. It doesn't use the advertising identifier.

Children

Spot On is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If Spot On ever changes how it handles information, this page will be updated before the change ships, with a new effective date.
